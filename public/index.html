Your monthly guide
  1. Export your form’s response sheet as a CSV or a ZIP containing the CSV. Keep the original column headers.
  2. Choose it under This month’s votes. Book titles are detected from the headers automatically.
  3. Review any warnings. Partial ballots should start at 1 and continue without gaps. Unranked books are tied last.
  4. Choose which attendance groups should count. All groups are included initially.
  5. Optionally add a past month, check the name matches, and turn on the returning-voters filter.
  6. Read the winner and ranking. If books tie, the app reports the tie for your club to resolve.

Nothing is saved after you leave or refresh. Keep your original exports. Corrections, links, and overrides apply only to this session. Use your browser’s Print command to save a result for your records.

Passing this on? Give the next organizer this website address and the original exports through your usual private channel. Monthly use needs no login. Code and maintenance instructions are in the club repository.
